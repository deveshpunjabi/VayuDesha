Data sources & limits

Included data

Regional modelled US AQI, six outdoor pollutants, 24-hour forecasts, surface wind dispersion, and your local household profile.

Not measured

Indoor PM2.5 levels, street-level pollution, personal inhaled dose, medical risk percentages, active GRAP orders, or personal CO₂ savings.

Live readings use Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS regional models reported on the US AQI scale, not official India CPCB station monitors.
